### Decision weights

- Quality Gates: 40%
- Evidence Coverage: 20%
- AI Readiness: 15%
- Business Readiness: 25%

### Risk policy

- LOW: readiness at least 85 with no failed gates
- MEDIUM: readiness from 70 through 84
- HIGH: readiness below 70 or any failed gate
