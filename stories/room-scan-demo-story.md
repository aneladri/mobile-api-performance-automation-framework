As a field technician,
I want to create a RoomScan session, capture room evidence, review capture quality,
upload the scan for AI processing, and make the generated floor plan available in the portal,
so that the inspection workflow can be reviewed and approved digitally.

Acceptance Criteria:
1. The technician can initialize a RoomScan session.
2. The application validates device and capture readiness.
3. The technician can capture living-room evidence.
4. Capture coverage and tracking confidence are validated.
5. The evidence is uploaded for AI processing.
6. A floor plan is generated and made available for portal review.
7. The portal user can review, approve, and submit the completed scan.
